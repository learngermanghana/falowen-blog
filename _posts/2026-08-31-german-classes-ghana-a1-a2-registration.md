## Current A1 German Classes

For learners starting German from the beginning, the current class selection includes **A1 Berlin Klasse** and **A1 Hamburg Klasse**.

A1 is the first CEFR level and is designed to help beginners build the language they need for simple everyday situations. Typical A1 skills include:

- introducing yourself and giving personal information;
- asking and answering basic questions;
- talking about family, hobbies, food, work and daily routines;
- understanding simple German notices and short conversations;
- writing short messages and emails;
- building the grammar and vocabulary needed for later Goethe exam preparation.

If you have never studied German before, an A1 class is normally the appropriate starting point.

## Already Finished A1? Continue with A2

Falowen also has an **upcoming A2 German class** for learners who already have an A1 foundation.

At A2, you move beyond isolated beginner sentences and learn to communicate in a wider range of everyday situations. You work on areas such as describing experiences, making arrangements, understanding longer everyday texts, writing more detailed messages and using German grammar with greater accuracy.

A2 is especially important because it creates the bridge between beginner German and the more independent communication expected at B1.

If you are unsure whether A1 or A2 is right for you, use Falowen's placement option or contact the school before registering.

## What Should You Check Before Choosing A1 or A2?

Do not choose a level simply because you recognize some German words. Your class should match what you can actually understand, write and say.

Choose **A1** if you are a complete beginner or still need to build the basic foundations of German. Choose **A2** if you have completed A1 and can already handle simple introductions, everyday questions, basic sentence structures and common beginner vocabulary.

Starting at the correct level makes it easier to follow lessons and make steady progress.
