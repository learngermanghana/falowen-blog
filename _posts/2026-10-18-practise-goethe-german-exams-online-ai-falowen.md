## Practise all four German exam skills

The Falowen Exams Room supports the four key skills tested in German-language exams:

- **Lesen (Reading):** Work through texts, questions and reading-comprehension exercises.
- **Hören (Listening):** Practise listening carefully and answering questions based on what you hear.
- **Schreiben (Writing):** Develop your written responses and receive AI-assisted assessment and suggestions for improvement.
- **Sprechen (Speaking):** Practise speaking with an AI examiner, either by focusing on a single part or working through a complete supported speaking-exam flow.

You can concentrate on a weaker skill or choose a fuller exam-style practice experience, depending on the level and exercises available in your account.

## What makes AI speaking practice different?

Speaking is often the part learners find hardest to practise alone. In Falowen's speaking area, you can select a particular exam part or start a complete supported speaking practice session.

For example, the A1 speaking practice includes introducing yourself, asking and answering questions, and making polite requests and responding to them. The full speaking option is designed to move through the parts and present results at the end.

That means you can become more comfortable responding in German before you sit down for an actual oral examination.

## Learn from your results, not just your mistakes

Completing a sample exam is only the beginning. Falowen uses automatic marking for supported objective tasks and AI-assisted assessment for eligible speaking and writing exercises.

Reviewing scores and feedback can help you identify what to practise next: perhaps reading more carefully, improving listening comprehension, structuring a written answer or speaking more confidently.

**AI scores are practice feedback, not official Goethe-Institut scores or a guarantee of passing.** Use them as guidance alongside continued study and, where available, support from a teacher.

## A simple way to prepare before exam day

For your next study session, try this routine:

1. Open the **Exams Room** in Falowen and select an appropriate level.
2. Choose a sample exam or practise one of the four skills.
3. Complete the exercise under the available exam-style conditions.
4. Review the score, corrections or feedback provided.
5. Return to the areas you found difficult and try again.

Regular practice can make the question formats feel more familiar and help you approach exam day with a clearer idea of what to expect.
