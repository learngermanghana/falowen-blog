German learning does not end when the live class finishes. You may want to check your next assignment, understand feedback from a previous workbook or prepare for tomorrow's German lesson. **Falowen students can now use the Falowen Learning Companion with ChatGPT**, where the connection is available to their ChatGPT account.

The idea is simple: you already have structured lessons, teachers, course books and assignments in Falowen. ChatGPT becomes an additional study partner that can help you ask questions about **your own authorized Falowen learning information**, instead of relying only on general advice about learning German.

This guide explains what you can do, how to get connected, and where to complete your official course work.

## What is the Falowen Learning Companion?

The **Falowen Learning Companion** is a connection between ChatGPT and Falowen, the online learning platform developed by Learn Language Education Academy in Ghana.

Depending on the tools available in the published connection and your course enrollment, you can use ChatGPT to:

- **Find your next assignment.** Ask which Falowen tutor assignments are recorded for your course and open the relevant Campus links.
- **Review recorded results and feedback.** Look at available tutor-marked scores and comments, then ask what to practise next.
- **Prepare for your next German class.** Where your verified timetable is available, get help reviewing the next lesson's topic and preparation tasks.
- **Explore your German lesson.** Ask for lesson goals, topic information or available learner-facing content from your enrolled level.
- **Practise German through conversation.** Ask for grammar explanations, vocabulary examples or short questions at your level.

The connection does not give ChatGPT permission to view another student's grades or invent information missing from your course record.

## How to connect Falowen to ChatGPT

1. **Have your own Falowen account.** If you are new, [create a Falowen account](https://www.falowen.app/signup) and select your course.
2. **Open [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/).** In the available Plugins or app connections, search for **Falowen Learning Companion**.
3. **Connect your account.** If the companion is available to you, follow the connection prompts and sign in through the authorized Falowen page.
4. **Start with a question.** For example, ask: **“Open my Falowen and show me my next assignment.”**

Availability can vary according to your ChatGPT account or workspace and the published companion version. If you cannot find the connection, continue studying in [Falowen Campus](https://www.falowen.app/campus); you do not need to share your password or private records in a chat message.

For a dedicated walkthrough, visit [**Learn German with ChatGPT: Falowen student guide**](https://www.falowen.app/learn-german-with-chatgpt).

## Six useful questions to try in ChatGPT

These are starting prompts you can adapt to your German level and current course:

1. **“Open my Falowen and check my next assignment.”**
2. **“Show my latest Falowen results and explain which areas need improvement.”**
3. **“Prepare me for my next live German class.”**
4. **“Show me the topic for Day 5 of my enrolled German course.”**
5. **“Help me practise the German vocabulary from my current lesson.”**
6. **“Ask me five short German questions at my level, one at a time.”**

Some prompts are ordinary ChatGPT practice and do not require personal data. Others need an authorized Falowen connection and may return only the records actually available. If an assignment has no recorded grade, that does not automatically mean it was not submitted.

## An example: using AI to understand tutor feedback

Suppose your tutor corrects the sentence:

*Ich schreibe Ihnen, weil ich brauche Hilfe.*

The corrected sentence is:

**Ich schreibe Ihnen, weil ich Hilfe brauche.**

You can ask ChatGPT: *“Why does ‘brauche’ move to the end after ‘weil’? Give me two more A1 examples and then test me.”*

This is more useful than simply copying an answer. You understand the grammar rule, practise it with new examples and then apply it in your own writing.

For an informal message, you might use **“Ich schreibe dir, weil ich …”** instead. In both cases, the conjugated verb belongs at the end of the *weil* clause.

## Can students from A1 to C2 use it?

Falowen provides German learning resources from **A1 to C2**, but not every course level has the same assignment, marking or ChatGPT features.

For example, students with tutor-marked A1–B1 assignments may have recorded grades and feedback to review. Advanced-level independent-study lessons may provide learning materials without a verified tutor-marked completion status. ChatGPT should tell you when information is unavailable rather than guess.

The most useful method is to combine three things: your official Falowen course materials, the guidance of your teachers, and short, regular ChatGPT practice.

## What ChatGPT does not replace

ChatGPT is a learning companion, **not your official examiner or assignment submission system**. You should still use Falowen Campus to submit workbooks, take timed assessments, attend classes and access officially recorded results.

Falowen's exam-style practice is independent learning material; it is not an official Goethe-Institut examination or certification service.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a Falowen account?

Yes, to connect and retrieve your personal Falowen records. You can still ask ChatGPT general German language questions without connecting your student account.

### Will ChatGPT automatically know my grades?

No. It needs an authorized Falowen connection with the relevant tool available, and it can only show records the connection can retrieve.

### Can ChatGPT submit my German workbook?

No. Complete and submit official assignments through Falowen Campus.

### Can I access the companion on my phone?

You can use ChatGPT and Falowen on supported mobile browsers or apps. Whether the Falowen connection is available depends on your ChatGPT account and supported experience.

### Does it support Swahili lessons too?

The connected learning-content tools described here focus on German. Swahili course access through the companion should not be assumed.

## Start using Falowen with ChatGPT

You do not have to wait until your next class to review your German. Check the work recorded for your course, practise a difficult grammar point and come to class with clearer questions.

**Your teacher and course remain at the centre of your learning. ChatGPT is an additional way to prepare and practise.**
