## How to Ask Simple Questions in German

Learning how to ask questions is a key step in speaking German confidently. Today, we'll focus on some of the most common **German question words** you’ll use to gather information in everyday conversations.

### Common German Question Words

Here are four essential question words to get you started:

- **Wo** – Where  
  *Use this to ask about locations.*  
  Beispiel: *Wo ist der Bahnhof?* (Where is the train station?)

- **Was** – What  
  *Use this to ask about things or actions.*  
  Beispiel: *Was machst du?* (What are you doing?)

- **Wer** – Who  
  *Use this to ask about people.*  
  Beispiel: *Wer ist das?* (Who is that?)

- **Wann** – When  
  *Use this to ask about time.*  
  Beispiel: *Wann beginnt der Film?* (When does the movie start?)

### More Examples of Simple Questions

- Wo wohnst du? (Where do you live?)  
- Was möchtest du essen? (What would you like to eat?)  
- Wer kommt heute Abend? (Who is coming tonight?)  
- Wann hast du Zeit? (When do you have time?)  
- Woher kommst du? (Where do you come from?)  
- Was kostet das? (What does that cost?)

### Quick Tips for Asking Questions

- Question words usually come at the beginning of the sentence.  
- The verb follows directly after the question word.  
- Keep your questions short and clear, especially when starting out.
