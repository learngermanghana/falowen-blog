## 20 Basic German Words Every Student Must Know

Learning basic German words is the first step to building a strong foundation in the language. These essential words will help you understand everyday conversations and express simple ideas clearly. Here are 20 important vocabulary items every beginner should memorize.

### 1. Common Greetings and Polite Words
- **Hallo** – Hello  
- **Bitte** – Please / You’re welcome  
- **Danke** – Thank you  
- **Tschüss** – Bye  

### 2. Essential Pronouns and Articles
- **Ich** – I  
- **Du** – You (informal)  
- **Der / Die / Das** – The (masculine / feminine / neuter)  

### 3. Basic Question Words
- **Wer?** – Who?  
- **Was?** – What?  
- **Wo?** – Where?  

### 4. Useful Everyday Verbs
- **Sein** – To be  
- **Haben** – To have  
- **Gehen** – To go  

### 5. Numbers and Time Indicators
- **Eins** – One  
- **Heute** – Today  
- **Morgen** – Tomorrow  

---

## Practical Tips for Learning Basic German Words

- **Use flashcards:** Write the German word on one side and the English meaning on the other. Review daily.  
- **Practice in context:** Try making simple sentences like *Ich bin müde* (I am tired) or *Wo ist das Buch?* (Where is the book?).  
- **Listen and repeat:** Hearing these words in conversations or audio lessons helps with pronunciation and retention.
