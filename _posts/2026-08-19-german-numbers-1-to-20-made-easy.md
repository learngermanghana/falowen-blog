## German Numbers 1 to 20 Made Easy

Learning numbers is one of the first steps when starting German. Today, we'll focus on **German numbers 1 to 20** with clear examples to help you remember them quickly.

---

### Practical Examples Using Numbers 1 to 20

- **Ich habe zwei Äpfel.**  
  (I have two apples.)

- **Sieben Tage in einer Woche.**  
  (Seven days in a week.)

- **Wir treffen uns um fünf Uhr.**  
  (We meet at five o’clock.)

- **Mein Bruder ist elf Jahre alt.**  
  (My brother is eleven years old.)

- **Ich kaufe vierzehn Bücher.**  
  (I buy fourteen books.)

- **Zwanzig Schüler sind im Klassenzimmer.**  
  (Twenty students are in the classroom.)

---

### Tips for Remembering German Numbers 1 to 20

- Notice that from 13 to 19, German numbers combine the base number + "zehn" (ten), similar to English "thirteen," "fourteen," etc.
- Pay special attention to pronunciation differences like **sieben** (seven) vs. **siebzehn** (seventeen).
- Practice counting aloud daily to build confidence.
